Thread Parameters

Custom dimensions use the selected thread system (in + TPI or mm + pitch) independently of the top display toggle. Metric “extra fine” groups smaller ISO/DIN fine pitches; ISO 261 does not formally define a separate extra-fine series.

Thread Calculations

Calculation assumptions

Basic 60° thread geometry only. No allowance or tolerance-class limits are applied. Cut-tap and form-tap predrill sizes use approximate Haas reference formulas; verify required minor diameter and tap maker recommendations, especially for forming taps.

Source: Haas tapping / threading formulas. Thread pitch series: ASME B1.1, ISO 261 and selected DIN 13 fine threads.

Uses the thread diameter and pitch selected above. Wire geometry assumes straight, symmetrical thread flanks.

Three-Wire Parameters

Wire Measurement Results

Measurement Method

Position two calibrated wires in adjacent grooves on one side and a third on the opposite side. Measure over all wires. Enter actual calibrated wire diameter.

Equation reference: Pratt & Whitney Measurement Systems. Lead-angle, wire deformation, measuring force, and flank-form corrections are not applied. Check drawing tolerance limits.

Haas macro variables & programs

The macro-variable tables are organized by machine control family. They describe documented variable numbers and ranges, not live values from your machine. User globals may be occupied by factory probing or automation. Do not overwrite variables without verifying their ownership on the actual machine.
